## Design Context

### Users
Founders and operators deciding whether to hand Jan a task and book a call; virtual assistants learning AI (the "For VAs" shelf); readers of the model tests.

### Brand Personality
Intelligent, precise, calm, in control. The promise is "Hand one task over. Keep the final say." Nothing goes out until the client clears it.

### Aesthetic Direction
Direction E, "Clearance board" (approved by Jan, 2026-11-02; system in `DESIGN.md`, tokens in `assets/css/e.css`). Monochrome: a black bay, white strips, three working greys. Big Shoulders Display for headings, Schibsted Grotesk for body, JetBrains Mono for meta. The logo's square and slanted bar are the only motifs. Product marks keep their colours inside white squares; nothing else is coloured.

### Design Principles
- One idea per page, told through the board: work runs, outward work waits for your OK, then it is cleared.
- One primary CTA per section; everything else is an underlined link.
- The logo never animates. Motion is transform/opacity/clip-path only, runs only in view, and is fully off under reduced motion with every piece of content still reachable.
- Illustrations are labelled as illustrations ("not live data"). No invented numbers, clients or quotes; unconfirmed content stays a visible "Jan to confirm" slot.
- Each blog post keeps its own story inside the shared post template; shelf decides the closing CTA.
- Budgets: LCP ≤ 2.5 s mobile, CLS ≤ 0.1, TBT ≤ 200 ms, WCAG 2.2 AA, visible focus on dark and light grounds.
