## Design Context

### Users
Portfolio visitors evaluating Jan's AI agents and automation work, including prospective clients deciding whether to explore projects or book a call.

### Brand Personality
Intelligent, precise, and futuristic. The site should build confidence and curiosity through clear language and a memorable visual experience.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark, restrained portfolio design with luminous near-white nanotechnology against the existing black background. The hero animation should feel organic, cinematic, and premium, while the rest of the interface stays easy to scan.

### Design Principles
- Give the hero one continuous visual story that visitors understand without controls.
- Make motion communicate transformation through scattering, orbiting, and reassembly.
- Preserve the site's existing logo silhouette and monochrome identity.
- Keep text, controls, and particles visible on desktop and mobile.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and provide a clear pause control.
