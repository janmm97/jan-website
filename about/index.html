I'm Jan. I connect operations, technology, and the way people work.

The person behind the systems

I've always believed in the idea of working smarter, not harder. Not by cutting corners, but by finding better ways to solve problems, simplify repetitive work, and spend more time on the things that actually matter.

That mindset is what drew me to technology, automation, and AI. I enjoy thinking critically, asking questions, and figuring out how things can be done more efficiently — whether that means improving a workflow, automating a tedious process, or simply finding a better way to get from point A to point B.

I'm a Computer Science graduate who enjoys building practical solutions that make everyday work a little easier. Over the years, I've worked as a Virtual Assistant, AI Agent Builder, and Automation Specialist, helping businesses and teams improve their workflows, automate repetitive tasks, and make better use of technology.

Today, I work as an Executive Assistant focused on Operations and AI Agents, combining operations, automation, and AI to solve real problems and help things run more smoothly.

This site is also a place for me to share what I learn along the way — from AI agents and automation to operations, tools, experiments, and lessons from the work I do.

If any of that interests you, feel free to look around. You might learn something useful — and hopefully, so will I. :D
