# Greek god references

These WebP assets are compressed copies of the three images supplied by the site owner for the Services hero:

- `Typing.webp`: `TypingGreekGod.png` (435 × 400)
- `Waving.webp`: `WavingGreekGod.png` (1312 × 1199)
- `PointingDown.webp`: `PointingDownGreekGod.png` (1357 × 1159)

The animation samples silhouette coordinates and luminance from these references. It does not display or crossfade the photographs during normal playback. The waving image also provides the static fallback if animation preparation fails.

The three assets total approximately 203 KB. Keep their dimensions unchanged unless the anatomical landmarks in `assets/js/service-logo.js` are updated too.
