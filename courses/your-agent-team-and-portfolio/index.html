What's inside

When you finish, you have a team of three tested agents on a schedule, a failure alert, a monthly check routine, and a one-page case study with real numbers. AI apps change often. App steps are dated (as of 2026-11).

  1. 8.1 Plan a team of three. Plan three agents with one job each, and draw what each one hands to the next. (20 min)
  2. 8.2 A lead agent and your approval gate. Write a lead brief that runs the team in order and stops at a human approval gate before any outward step. (25 min)
  3. 8.3 Run it on a schedule. Choose the right scheduling option for your team and set up a weekday morning run. (25 min)
  4. 8.4 Know when it fails. Add a daily health line and a failure alert so you learn about a broken run the same morning. (20 min)
  5. 8.5 Write an honest case study. Write a one-page case study of your agent team using only real, dated numbers from your own logs. (25 min)
  6. 8.6 Talk to clients about agents. Explain an agent offer to a client in plain words, with clear limits, controls and no promises of results. (20 min)
  7. 8.7 Your monthly tool check. Set up a 30-minute monthly check that keeps your agents, costs and course notes up to date. (15 min)
