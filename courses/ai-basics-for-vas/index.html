The in-between step

You already use ChatGPT. But the answers often sound generic, and you spend time fixing them before a client sees them.

When you look for help, you find two kinds of lessons. Short videos that only show the chat box. Or long courses made for developers, full of code.

There is not much in between for a VA who wants to do good work with AI, step by step, without code. This course is that in-between step.

What you'll be able to do

After Course 1, you can:

  • Write prompts that give you clear, useful answers the first time more often.
  • Use templates and simple structured prompts (like JSON) for tasks you repeat every week.
  • Set up the AI to write in your style or your client's style, with instructions and examples.
  • Pick ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini for a task, and say why.
  • Check AI work for mistakes before you send it to a client.
  • Decide what client information is safe to put into an AI tool, and what is not.
  • Explain what an AI model, tokens and context are, in plain words, to a client.
  • Understand what an AI agent is, and how it is different from a chat.

Who it's for

This course is for you if:

  • You are a virtual assistant: an executive assistant, admin VA or operations VA.
  • You feel behind on AI, or you use it but get generic answers.
  • You have no tech background. You don't need one.
  • You want to start on a free AI plan before you pay for anything.

This course is not for you if:

  • You are a developer who wants to use the AI API. That comes in later courses in the series.
  • You want screen-recording video tutorials. This course is an interactive lesson file with narration, not screen demos.
  • You are looking for a way to get rich fast. This course teaches skills. It does not promise income.
  • You need a certificate. No certificate is included.

What's inside

  1. 1. Your first AI win. Use a simple prompt pattern and get a better answer in about 20 minutes.
  2. 2. What an LLM really is. How AI chat tools work, and why they sometimes get things wrong.
  3. 3. Tokens, context windows and memory. What the AI can "see" and remember in a chat, and what it forgets.
  4. 4. Thinking models and reasoning effort. When to let the AI think longer, and when a quick answer is fine.
  5. 5. Types of prompting. Simple ways to ask, with examples you can use at work.

  9. 9. ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini. What each one does well, so you can choose.
  10. 10. Check before you send. A simple way to catch mistakes and made-up facts.
  11. 11. Client data, privacy and AI ethics. What to share with AI, what to keep out, and how to be honest about using it.
  12. 12. A taste of agents. A first look at AI that can take steps for you.
